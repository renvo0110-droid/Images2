The Power of Your Subconscious Mind

This book taught me that the way I think is just as important as how hard I work. It’s like mental training for life – the same way athletes train their bodies.

If you’ve ever felt stuck, doubtful, or afraid to take the next step, this book shows you how to reprogram those thoughts from the inside out.

What the Book is Really About

The book explains that you have two minds: the conscious mind (the part that plans, decides and worries) and the subconscious mind (the part that quietly controls habits, reactions and results).

Whatever you repeatedly think, imagine and emotionally feel sinks into your subconscious – and your subconscious starts working day and night to make those ideas real.

Key Ideas I Learned

  • Your subconscious is always listening to your thoughts and self-talk.
  • Affirmations and repeated thoughts slowly reprogram old beliefs.
  • Fear, doubt and “I can’t” are also programs – and they can be replaced.
  • Visualization sends a clear “mental picture” of what you want.
  • Gratitude and faith keep your mind strong when life is tough.

How This Book Changed Me

Before this book, I didn’t realise how negative my inner voice was. I would often think “What if I fail?” or “I’m not good enough.”

After reading it, I became more aware of my self-talk and started replacing it with stronger beliefs:

  • “I am capable of learning hard things.”
  • “I can handle pressure calmly.”
  • “I improve a little bit every day.”

My Mindset Playbook (How I Use It Daily)

  • Morning: 2–3 minutes of affirmations about my goals and strengths.
  • During the day: Whenever I catch a negative thought, I pause and replace it.
  • Evening: Visualise a confident, successful version of myself.
  • Weekly: Write 3–5 things I am grateful for and what I did well.

Why This Book Might Help You

If you are someone who:

  • Overthinks and doubts yourself a lot
  • Wants more confidence in studies, sports or career
  • Feels stuck in old habits or patterns
  • Loves self-improvement and mindset growth

…then this book can give you a simple but powerful way to start changing your inner story.

If You Want to Start Today

  • Choose one positive affirmation about yourself and repeat it daily.
  • Visualise one specific goal (exam, project, fitness, etc.) for a few minutes.
  • Notice when you think “I can’t” and gently switch it to “I’m learning how.”

Small, consistent mental reps – just like training – create big changes over time.
